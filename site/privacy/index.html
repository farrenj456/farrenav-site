Travel Compare privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Travel Compare is a private tool built and used by Joey Farren, a freelance AV technician, to keep track of his own work bookings and travel costs. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. This page explains what it does with data, including data from Google accounts.

What it accesses

  • Gmail. It reads recent emails in Joey's own Gmail account to spot work enquiries, bookings and cancellations. When Joey accepts an enquiry made on his availability page, it can send one reply to that client from his Gmail, and only after Joey has read the email and tapped Send. It cannot delete, label or change any email.
  • Availability enquiries. The name, company, role, email address and dates a client enters in the enquiry form on this site are passed to Joey's private Telegram bot so he can reply. They are not used for anything else.
  • Calendar. It adds, updates and removes events for Joey's own jobs in his own Apple iCloud calendars.
  • Messages and notes he sends it. Text, forwarded messages, voice-memo transcripts and photos that Joey sends to his own private Telegram bot.
  • Locations. Job addresses, used with Google Maps to work out driving and public transport times and costs.

How it is used

Data is used only to find job details (company, dates, location, rate), keep Joey's job list and calendar up to date, and compare travel costs. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared for any other purpose.

Who processes it

  • Anthropic (Claude API) reads the text of relevant emails and messages to pull out job details.
  • Google provides Gmail access and Maps routing.
  • Telegram carries messages between Joey and his bot.
  • Apple iCloud stores the calendar events.
  • The hosting provider runs the private server where the job list is kept.

Each processes data only to provide its service.

Storage and security

The job list is stored on a private server that only Joey's own devices and Telegram account can reach, using secret access tokens. Email content is not stored in full; only the job details taken from it and a short reference to the email are kept. Joey can delete any job, and its calendar events, at any time.

Google user data

Travel Compare's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Gmail data is not used to develop, improve or train general AI or machine-learning models, and is not transferred to anyone except as needed to provide the features described above.

Access can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
